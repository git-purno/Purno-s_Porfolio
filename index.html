Currently doing independent research in post-quantum cryptography, alongside my bachelor's coursework. Short version below, with the option to go deeper.

Independent Research In Progress

Beyond Key Exchange: A Framework for Post-Quantum Certificate Migration in Regulated Financial Infrastructure

Design Science Research · Post-Quantum Cryptography · Financial Infrastructure

Quantum computers will eventually break the encryption the internet runs on. The industry has already started fixing how systems exchange keys — but almost no one has touched how systems prove their identity through certificates, and banks are the most exposed part of that gap. This research builds a staged, risk-based framework to close it.

the_gap

Roughly half of measured internet domains have already moved to post-quantum key exchange. Post-quantum certificates — the part that proves a server's identity — sit at almost 0% adoption on the same domains, and banking is measured as one of the slowest sectors to move on either front, despite holding the most sensitive, long-lived data.

research_objectives

  • RO1 — Design a staged, risk-based migration roadmap built specifically around how regulated banks actually operate.
  • RO2 — Build a decision model that matches each hybrid-certificate design (Composite, Catalyst, Chameleon) to a system's risk level, bandwidth limits, and regulatory exposure.
  • RO3 — Propose and test a way to verify that a hybrid certificate's dual-security promise is actually enforced after deployment, not just assumed.

migration_path

  • Stage 1 — Hybrid leaf certificates, scheme chosen to fit the system.
  • Stage 2 — Hybrid CA chains, coordinated with certificate authorities.
  • Stage 3 — Full post-quantum certificates with updated revocation checks, classical signatures retired.
